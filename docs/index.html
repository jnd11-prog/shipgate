Shipgate

A statistically honest release gate for LLM features.

Teams often decide a prompt change is "better" from one run of a few dozen examples. Shipgate measures the three things that make that decision unreliable: randomness in the model, too few test cases, and mistakes by the AI grader. It then returns SHIP or BLOCKED.

Demo verdict for v2: BLOCKED. A single-run eval scores it at 92% and ships it.

What the demo found

  • The gain may be luck. v2 improves on v1 by 17 points, but the paired test gives p = 0.69, so it isn't statistically significant.
  • A hidden regression. v2's reply to an angry customer is dismissive in 1 of 5 tries. One try per case would likely miss it.
  • A made-up fact. v2 quotes the wrong price for a plan.
  • The suite is too small. With 12 cases the pass rate could be anywhere from 55% to 95%. Certifying an 85% bar takes at least 22 cases.
  • The grader isn't reliable yet. It catches only 67% of bad replies (κ = 0.50), so Shipgate marks it NOT TRUSTED.
